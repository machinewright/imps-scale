The Imp’s Scale

An Imp holds the scale. It hasn't decided which weight is heavier than which, and it never will until it has to. Each time you weigh two, it picks the answer that hurts you most, without ever contradicting itself. Each level gives you exactly as many weighings as the best possible strategy needs. Not one more.

    How it works

    The Imp has no secret answer. After every weighing it looks at all the ways the weights could still be ordered, and at every strategy you could still follow, and picks the answer that leaves you worst off. So winning a level means having a plan that works whatever it says. Lose, and the game shows the weighing where your plan stopped being safe.

    The weights board: the row a weight sits in is the number of weights known to be heavier than it, so the top row holds every weight that might still be the heaviest. A line from a higher weight to a lower one means the higher one is known to be heavier.

    The Imp plays perfectly: every answer comes from an exact search over all strategies, so a level can't be won by luck, only by a strategy that works whatever it says. Your progress stays in this browser.
